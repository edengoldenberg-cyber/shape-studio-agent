These rules were set by the studio owner. Where the extracted guidance below disagrees, these win.

- **Language:** all on-screen text, cover text and captions are in Hebrew.
- **Existing footage, not a new script:** you are selecting from what a person already said. The
  guidance about hooks and structure is for judging WHICH part of the recording works as a short
  video — never for rewriting, tightening or re-ordering their words.
- **Exact quotation:** on-screen cards and the cover quote the speaker word for word. A testimonial
  must keep its meaning; never cut it so that it claims more than the speaker said.
- **No scores:** hook scores, "human" scores and readiness scores are not supported for Hebrew and
  are not evidence of quality. Do not produce, estimate or mention any score.
- **No performance predictions:** do not say or imply that a video will get views, reach or results.
- **No invented brand voice:** no voice profile has been approved. Write captions plainly, in
  ordinary spoken Hebrew, without slogans or a "brand tone".
- **No unverified business facts:** no prices, offers, discounts, schedules, class lists, numbers of
  members, results, guarantees or health claims unless they appear in the verified brand facts you
  were given. If a call to action is not provided there, the caption has none.
- **Durations:** use only the timestamps in the transcript. Do not estimate speaking time from
  word counts.
- **Two lines of business** (strength training, reformer Pilates) are separate; do not assume a
  statement about one applies to the other.
- **The opening seconds:** prefer a segment whose first sentence already says something concrete
  (a claim, a fear, a change), over one that needs a warm-up — but only if the thought stays whole.
- **Hashtags:** none in this phase.
